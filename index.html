Why I build

I started coding looking for a way to turn ideas into something people could actually click on and use. A first-year CSE timetable is mostly fundamentals — but every spare hour goes into a side project, a HackerRank challenge, or making one more line of my résumé true instead of aspirational.

Outside of code, I write — tech explainers, short blog posts, and the occasional piece on what it's actually like learning to program from scratch. Public speaking and content creation feel like the same skill as debugging: breaking a confusing thing down until someone else can follow it.
